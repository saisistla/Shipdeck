# Gate register

## Problem
Parents at the school gate need a register that shows which child has been collected today. The paper sheet is hard to read when two teachers are releasing children at once.

## Who it is for
Teachers and the front office of one primary school.

## Offer
A single page that lists today's class and lets a teacher mark one child as collected.

## Version one
- Show today's class list.
- Mark one child as collected.
- Keep grades, fees, and parent messages out of this page.

## Not in version one
- Grades.
- School fees.
- Messages to parents.

## Assumptions
- The first version uses sample pupil names, not the live school roll.
- One school will try it for a week before anyone talks about a price.
